The Idea Funnel — How Pipelines Work

Innovation pipelines convert a broad pool of raw ideas into a small set of developed initiatives. Ideas are progressively filtered through structured gates, with fewer ideas advancing at each stage.

Stevens & Burley (1997) famously showed it takes roughly 3,000 raw ideas to produce one successful commercial product. The ratio varies by industry, but the principle holds: pipelines must be wide at the top.

Idea Collection Methods

The first stage of any innovation pipeline is generating and capturing ideas. Organizations use a range of structured and unstructured approaches to fill the top of the funnel.

Best practice: Don't filter during collection. The goal at this stage is quantity over quality. Premature filtering causes survivorship bias and kills unconventional ideas before they can be explored.
